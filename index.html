CueScroll Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

CueScroll (“we”, “the app”) is a teleprompter utility for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what data we collect and how it is used.

Data we collect

CueScroll does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

  • Scripts you create are stored only on your device.
  • Purchase status is handled by Apple through StoreKit / App Store. We do not receive your payment card details.

How we use data

On-device scripts are used solely to provide teleprompter functionality. We do not sell personal data. We do not track users across apps or websites.

Data sharing

We do not share script content with third parties. Apple may process purchase receipts as part of App Store commerce.

Data retention & deletion

Deleting the app removes local scripts from that device. You can also delete individual scripts inside the app. To remove a subscription, manage it in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Children

CueScroll is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
